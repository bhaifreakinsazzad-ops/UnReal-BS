Communication job
By the end, a non-technical Bangla-speaking reader should understand what every UNREAL BS feature does, how it works, what value it creates, which system powers it, and whether it is live, controlled, dark, read-only, or coming soon.

Narrative
1. What UNREAL BS is and how the system fits together.
2. HighLevel-powered customer and operations features.
3. UNREAL BS custom money, AI, service, and delivery features.
4. Platform commerce and advertising capabilities.
5. Security, administration, and operational truth.
6. Status map and practical knowledge-base usage.

Visual direction
- 16:9 widescreen, black-charcoal canvas with warm gold, purple, emerald, and cyan accents.
- Nirmala UI for Bengali readability.
- Infographic compositions: one large flow or relationship per feature, not dense UI-card mosaics.
- Plain-language Bengali with English product names where they are already part of the interface.
- Every slide contains a verified status label and a short source block in speaker notes.

Source truth
- Current repository routes, components, API handlers, README, ARCHITECTURE, QA_REPORT, and PRE_AD_LAUNCH_RUNBOOK.
- No invented usage numbers, growth claims, customer outcomes, or production activations.
